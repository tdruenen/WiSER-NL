# Disclaimer

## Purpose

WiSER-NL is provided as a scientific and engineering software tool for the determination of wind statistics and effective roughness lengths for wind climate assessments.

The software has been developed within the context of research and standardization activities related to NEN 8100:2027 and NPR 6097:2027.
